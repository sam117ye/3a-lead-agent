# Stage 2: seed manufacturing profile

$company_context

Task: build a company-level manufacturing profile of the boat or vessel manufacturer below. It is a seed company for 3A's search for new clients: it may already buy sandwich core materials, or it may be an aspirational target that does not use them yet.

Company: $company
Website: $website

Research the company's own website (model pages, construction and technology pages, brochures), press releases, boat-show coverage, trade press (composites and boatbuilding magazines) and supplier case studies.

Fill in:
- manufacturer: name, website and location (headquarters and main shipyards).
- end_products: the vessels or components they build (model ranges, boat types, sizes).
- manufacturing_processes: how they build hulls, decks and structures (for example vacuum infusion, hand lay-up, RTM, prepreg, thermoforming, CNC kitting, modular assembly).
- material_stack: what they build with: cores, reinforcements, resins, veils and infusion media, with brand or product names when disclosed.
- production_constraints: limits on their production (labour, cycle time, yard capacity, cost, regulations).
- performance_mandates: targets they commit to (weight, stiffness, speed or range, comfort, sustainability, recyclability).
- core_engineering_challenge: in one or two sentences, the main engineering challenge where sandwich composites could help them.

Rules:
- Use short phrases close to the source wording, for example "vacuum infusion of hull and deck" or "recycled PET foam core".
- Only report what the sources support. Leave a list empty when the information is not disclosed: an empty list means undisclosed, not absent.
