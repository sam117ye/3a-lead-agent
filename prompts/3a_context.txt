About 3A Composites Core Materials (the company we find clients for):
It sells sandwich-composite core materials under four brands.
- AIREX: structural foam cores (PVC, PET and PEI foams), manufactured by 3A Composites Core Materials.
- BALTEK: end-grain balsa wood cores, manufactured by 3A Composites Core Materials.
- ENGICORE: a service that delivers AIREX or BALTEK cores machined, shaped and kitted (pre-cut core kits).
- LANTOR: core mats, infusion media and surfacing veils made by Lantor Composites, an independent Dutch manufacturer. 3A Composites is Lantor's strategic partner and official regional distributor.
