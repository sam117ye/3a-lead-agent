# Stage 1: 3A product taxonomy

$company_context

Task: build the complete, current product dictionary of 3A Composites Core Materials.

Research these websites thoroughly, including product pages, product finders and technical datasheets (PDF):
- https://www.3accorematerials.com (AIREX, BALTEK, ENGICORE)
- https://www.lantorcomposites.com (LANTOR)

Brand rules:
- AIREX and BALTEK products are manufactured by 3A Composites Core Materials.
- ENGICORE is a kitting service (machined, shaped and kitted AIREX or BALTEK cores). List each ENGICORE offering as a product.
- LANTOR products are made by Lantor Composites and distributed by 3A Composites.

Return one entry per product line (for example one entry per foam grade, not one per density or sheet size). For each product:
- product_id: the official product name as the manufacturer writes it, for example "AIREX T92".
- brand: AIREX, BALTEK, LANTOR or ENGICORE.
- active: "yes" if currently offered, "no" if discontinued, "unknown" if unclear.
- material_composition: what the product is made of, in the source's own words.
- structural_mechanics: key mechanical properties (density range, shear and compressive strength, stiffness, impact and fatigue behaviour), with numbers where the source gives them.
- engineering_tolerances: temperature limits, compatible resins and processes (vacuum infusion, prepreg, RTM, thermoforming, hand lay-up), and other processing limits.

Use only information the sources support. Leave a field empty when the sources say nothing about it.
